# AI Coding Assistant Market Landscape - Evaluation Fixture

## Market
The fixture organizes the market around AI-first editors, IDE assistants, and enterprise development platforms.

## Competition
Products are compared across completion, chat, repository understanding, agent execution, IDE coverage, governance, and deployment options.

## Customers
Individual developers emphasize speed and usability, while enterprise buyers also require security, administration, auditability, and cost control.

## Pricing
Common commercial models include individual subscriptions, per-seat team plans, and negotiated enterprise contracts.

## Roadmap
Evaluation priorities include stronger agent reliability, broader tool integration, measurable productivity, and safer enterprise controls.

## Limitations
Market claims should be validated against first-party product documentation because features, packaging, and prices change frequently.
